Our Story

Building Uganda's Digital Future Since Day One

Founded in Kampala, NexaTech Solutions Uganda emerged from a clear observation: individuals, schools, and growing businesses struggle to find reliable, professional, and holistic technical support under one roof.

We set out to bridge that gap. Today, our multidisciplinary team of certified technicians, network engineers, and web developers provide dependable end-to-end IT services across the country—ensuring our clients operate securely and efficiently in an increasingly digital world.
